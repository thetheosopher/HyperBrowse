## 1. The big one: send an image to a folder without leaving the keyboard

This is my number one request by a distance. Right now, if I'm in full screen looking at pictures, there's no quick way to send one anywhere — I have to come out, find it, and go through a menu. That breaks the whole flow, and it's the single thing keeping this from being my daily driver.

**1.1 — IrfanView parity: `F7` = move, `F8` = copy.**
Burned into my hands after years, and as far as I can tell both keys are currently unused.

**1.2 — Let me assign my own shortcut key to each Quick Send destination.**
IrfanView gives each quick-send slot a key. I'd rather assign them myself — a small field on each Quick Send row where I set its shortcut.

Put together, that's the whole workflow: press `F7` (move) or `F8` (copy), a small popup shows my Quick Send folders with the key I assigned each one, and I press it. `F7`, `1` moves to Quick Send folder 1. `F8`, `1` copies there. Two keystrokes, no mouse.

Two things that matter for it:

- **The part that matters most to me is having it work in the viewer, including full screen.** That's where I am when I'm deciding where a picture belongs.
- **The popup is what makes it usable.** It means I don't have to remember all my keys cold — I'd learn the two or three I use hourly and still see the rest. Escape cancels.

That would make this the fastest culling workflow I've used anywhere.
